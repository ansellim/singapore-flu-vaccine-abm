# Model-ready Queenstown inputs used for the reported extension runs

Produced by `model/scripts/data/derive_queenstown.R` (with the case-insensitive
subzone match), 19 November 2026; `manifest.json` lists input and output
hashes. Tertiary sites: Anglo-Chinese Junior College, the National University
of Singapore (Kent Ridge) campus and Singapore Polytechnic. Venues: 10 hawker
centers, 5 community clubs and 14 licensed supermarket sites.

`../model_v1_superseded/` holds the inputs of a first run that was superseded:
they lacked the university campus and the supermarkets.
